I don’t always code. Sometimes, I run. And when I do, I like to reflect on it. That's how my "running" repository came to life. It's not your typical software project. Instead, it’s more of a digital journal where I document my thoughts, goals, and experiences related to running. 

## Why This Repo Exists

I started this repo because I wanted to track my personal journey in running—not just distances and times, but the insights I gather along the way. Focused reflections help me stay mindful about my running technique, breathing, and even how I prevent injuries. This isn’t just about logging data; it’s about improving how I approach the sport.

## Key Design Decisions

- **Personal Reflections Over Metrics**: Instead of creating a full-blown app to track stats, I opted for a more introspective approach. Running is as mental as it is physical. Documenting my thoughts helps me hone my technique.
  
- **Markdown Simplicity**: I chose Markdown for its ease of use. I didn't want the overhead of a framework complicating my notes. It's lightweight, straightforward, and allows for quick updates.

- **Static Content**: The structure is simple, providing a clean reading experience. This project isn't about dynamic content; it’s a collection of static reflections that anyone can browse.

## Tech Stack

This project uses a basic tech stack:
- **Markdown**: For content creation. It's perfect for jotting down thoughts without distractions.
- **Static Content**: No complex frameworks or languages. Just pure Markdown.

## Future Work / Roadmap

I do have plans for expanding this project. Here’s what I’m considering:

- **Analysis Tools**: If I feel brave enough to turn this into more of a software application, I may add tools for analysis and tracking. Think graphs or comparison charts that visualize progress over time.

This repository is essentially a simple and personal project right now, but it holds a lot of potential. I like the idea of it evolving based on my journey and my experiences. For now, the focus is on cultivating awareness around my running habits and improving my technique.
